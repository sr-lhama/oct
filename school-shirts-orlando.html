Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order?

Our standard minimum is 12 pieces. Tell us your quantities, styles, colors and size breakdown.

Can we use our school logo or mascot?

Yes, with authorization from the rights holder. Send your artwork or describe your idea.

Can we order youth and adult sizes together?

Let us know the size breakdown. We will confirm compatible styles, pricing and availability.

How long does production take?

Normally 7–10 business days after artwork approval and full payment. Rush orders are subject to availability and fees.

How are group prices determined?

We quote based on garment, size, artwork, print placement, quantity and deadline. Shipping is quoted separately.

Let’s make your school proud.

Tell us your school name, event date, quantities and artwork ideas. We’ll help you build the right order.
