Terms of Service

Basic policies for custom apparel estimates, approvals and production.

Requests and estimates

Submitting a website form is a request for an estimate, not a confirmed purchase. Our standard minimum is 12 pieces. Prices are reviewed after confirming garment styles, quantities, print positions, artwork requirements and availability. A quote is not final until Orlando Custom Tees approves and sends it.

Artwork and authorization

Customers confirm they own or have permission to use the names, logos and images submitted. The final print layout, placement, colors and artwork versions must be approved before production. An illustrative website preview is not an approved production proof. Revisions after approval may affect pricing and timing.

Payment

We require full payment before production begins. Payment instructions are provided with the approved order through an authorized payment method. A request or estimate alone does not authorize charges.

Production timelines

Typical production is 7–10 business days after final artwork approval and receipt of full payment, subject to product availability and the schedule stated in the approved estimate. A requested deadline is not guaranteed unless confirmed by our team. Rush fees, if applicable, are quoted and agreed before purchase.

Delivery and shipping

Local pickup may be arranged. Shipping charges are quoted separately and are not automatically included when you request an estimate. Please enter a complete and accurate delivery address. Shipping schedules are subject to the carrier and fulfillment conditions.

Cancellations, refunds and customized goods

Custom-made and decorated products may not be eligible for return or cancellation after artwork approval or production starts. Requests for changes, cancellations or refunds are reviewed according to the stage of the order, costs already incurred, the approved estimate and applicable law. Do not assume a refund has been approved until confirmed in writing.

Customer-supplied goods

We do not automatically accept garments supplied by customers. Materials must be reviewed and approved by Orlando Custom Tees before we agree to decorate them.

Customer account and reorders

A first estimate request does not require a login. Verified customers may view saved requests, request changes while a quote remains new and place a reorder using previous order details or artwork. A reorder creates a new request and is subject to current pricing, availability and approval.

Privacy and contact

Read our Privacy Policy for details on how we process order information and artwork. Questions about an order: order@orlandocustomtees.com.

Last updated November 15, 2026. The approved written estimate and any applicable contract govern specific transactions. These website terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel.
