Terms of Service

Basic policies for custom apparel estimates, approvals and production.

Quotes and pricing

Estimates are provided after reviewing garments, artwork, sizes, quantity and print locations. Prices, product availability and timelines are subject to confirmation.

Artwork and payment

Production starts only after final artwork approval and payment in full. The customer confirms they have permission to use submitted logos or images.

Production and delivery

Our standard production expectation is 7–10 business days after artwork approval and payment. Delivery and shipping costs are quoted separately. Rush deadlines and fees require explicit confirmation.

Garments and decoration

Customer-supplied garments are not accepted automatically. We assess suitability case by case and reserve the right to decline unsuitable materials.

Contact

Questions about your estimate or order: order@orlandocustomtees.com.
