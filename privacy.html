Privacy Policy

How Orlando Custom Tees handles the information you provide through this website.

On this page

  • Information we collect
  • How we use your information
  • Where information is stored
  • Account and artwork security
  • Data retention and requests
  • Communications
  • Cookies and browser storage
  • Updates and contact

Information we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name when provided, product selections, sizes, delivery information, notes and any artwork you choose to upload. We also collect the information needed to operate a secure customer account, keep order histories and respond to messages you send through the portal. Technical information may be processed to prevent fraud and abuse.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, prepare estimates, confirm artwork, fulfill orders, manage customer service, and allow you to access or reorder previous requests. We do not sell customer personal information or artwork. We do not use uploaded logos or designs in advertising or for unrelated purposes without permission.

Where information is stored

Estimate submissions, account records, private artwork files and portal messages are handled using Supabase. Authorized service providers may process information necessary to operate these services. We may share limited information with payment, manufacturing or delivery providers when required to fulfill an approved order. We do not sell customer data to those providers.

Account and artwork security

Customer accounts use secure email sign-in links. Order details and files are restricted to verified account holders and authorized Orlando Custom Tees staff. Artwork access uses temporary links when available. Please do not share your sign-in links with anyone.

Data retention and requests

We retain business and order records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with applicable legal or accounting obligations and resolve disputes. To request access, a correction or deletion of eligible personal information, contact us at info@orlandocustomtees.com. Some information may need to be retained for legal, accounting or security purposes.

Communications

By submitting an estimate request, you agree to receive messages necessary to respond to your request and service your order. Marketing communications are not required for an estimate. Messages sent within the customer portal are retained with the corresponding request. External email inbox synchronization is not currently part of the portal.

Cookies and browser storage

This website uses essential browser storage to remember your language, keep you signed in to your customer account and save your cookie choice.

The site saves your cookie choice in this browser for 180 days. You can change it at any time through Cookie settings in the footer. Clearing this site’s browser data also resets your choice.

If Google Analytics is enabled, it is loaded only after you accept optional cookies. It helps us understand page usage and whether a form was submitted. We do not send the contents of your forms to Google Analytics.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our services change. Privacy inquiries can be sent to info@orlandocustomtees.com.

Last updated November 15, 2026. This policy describes our current website and portal workflow; it should be reviewed with qualified legal counsel for applicable privacy obligations.
