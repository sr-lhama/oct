Privacy Policy

How Orlando Custom Tees handles the information you provide through this website.

Information we collect

When you request a quote we collect your contact details, garment selections and artwork files so we can evaluate and respond to your project. We may also collect basic technical information for security and performance.

How we use information

We use information for answering inquiries, preparing estimates, communicating about orders and supporting our services. Uploaded artwork is used only for legitimate order-related purposes unless you authorize other use.

Storage and providers

Information submitted to this version is stored on the server that hosts the website. Third-party providers involved in payments, shipping or email may process relevant data when those services are connected. We do not claim that every optional integration is already enabled.

Contact

For information inquiries or deletion requests please write to info@orlandocustomtees.com.
