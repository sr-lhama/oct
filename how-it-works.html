FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

Everything you should know before placing a custom apparel order.

What is the minimum order?

Our standard minimum is 12 pieces. Exceptions require confirmation before quoting.

How long does production take?

Most standard orders take approximately 7–10 business days after artwork approval and full payment. Larger or complex projects may take longer.

Is the digital mockup free?

Yes, a digital mockup is included. Complex artwork cleanup, redesign or file reconstruction may incur additional charges disclosed in the estimate.

Can I mix sizes?

Yes, size mixes can be requested within a design and production setup. Extended sizes may have different costs.

Can I supply my own garments?

We do not automatically accept customer-supplied garments. Ask first so we can assess material compatibility and production risk.

Do you offer embroidery?

Embroidery is not currently offered. Our primary decoration service is full-color DTF printing.

Can I request a rush order?

Rush requests are reviewed case by case, based on current workload, garment availability and the deadline. Rush fees may apply.

How do I pay?

Payment is required in full before production begins. Your invoice and payment instructions are provided after the order is approved.

Do you ship?

Shipping is available for applicable orders and is quoted separately. Ask us about local pickup and delivery options.

Can I get the artwork file?

We accept your artwork uploads. A digital apparel mockup for approval is included; original design or vector file creation is a separate service if needed.
