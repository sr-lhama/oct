What’s the best fabric for custom T-shirts?

Start with who will wear the shirt, where they’ll wear it, and how often it will be washed.

Cotton for events

For volunteers, event staff, or merchandise, start with the Gildan 64000 Softstyle. Many solid colors are ring-spun cotton, which has a soft feel and gives you a smooth surface for the print. Fiber content varies by color.

Cotton-poly blend for everyday wear

The Next Level 6210 CVC blends combed ring-spun cotton with polyester. It’s a good starting point when your staff want a soft shirt they can wear throughout the week.

Performance polyester for active crews

The Team 365 TT11 is a lightweight, moisture-wicking option for crews working outdoors or on the move. If your team needs a collar for customer-facing work, consider the Team 365 TT21 performance polo.

Prepare your logo for DTF printing

Send the original logo file when you have it. A vector PDF is a good starting point and can be uploaded directly with your quote request. A high-resolution PNG with a transparent background also works well for artwork that should print without a rectangle behind it.

Tell us where the design goes and the approximate size. A logo that looks clear across the back may need thicker lines or larger lettering when printed on the chest. We’ll flag details that need attention, such as thin outlines, tiny text, or a background that may show in the print.

What goes into your custom apparel quote?

Send the garment style or intended use, color, quantity by size, artwork, print locations, deadline, and pickup or shipping preference. The standard minimum is 12 pieces. Garment choice, extended sizes, print size and placement, artwork preparation, and rush timing can affect the estimate. Shipping is quoted separately.

Example for a 12-shirt work crew

Request 12 navy performance shirts in mixed sizes, with a small left-chest logo and a larger back print. Include both artwork files and the date the crew needs the shirts. That gives us the details to quote the garments and both print placements.

Review your mockup before we print

Your order includes a digital apparel mockup. Check the garment style and color, spelling, logo version, approximate print size, and placement. For a front-and-back order, review both sides. Tell us what needs changing before you approve it.

Standard production typically takes 7–10 business days after artwork approval and full payment. Let us know the date you need the order in hand, especially when it will be shipped.

From DTF transfer to finished shirt

Direct-to-film (DTF) printing puts the design onto a transfer film. A heat press then applies the transfer to the garment. We review the fabric and artwork together so the print suits the piece and placement.

A small chest logo on a polo and a large multicolor graphic on a T-shirt have different needs. See both examples below, or learn more about DTF printing.

How to wash and care for DTF-printed shirts

Follow the garment’s care label and any instructions included with your order.

Wash inside out

Turn the shirt inside out and use cold water and mild detergent. Wash with similar colors and avoid bleach on the print.

Dry gently

Air-dry when practical. If the label allows machine drying, use a low-heat setting.

Iron around the print

If the garment label permits ironing, turn the shirt inside out and keep the iron away from the printed area.

FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

Everything you should know before placing a custom apparel order.

What is the minimum order?

Our standard minimum is 12 pieces. Exceptions require confirmation before quoting.

How long does production take?

Most standard orders take approximately 7–10 business days after artwork approval and full payment. Larger or complex projects may take longer.

Is the digital mockup free?

Yes, a digital mockup is included. Complex artwork cleanup, redesign or file reconstruction may incur additional charges disclosed in the estimate.

Can I mix sizes?

Yes, size mixes can be requested within a design and production setup. Extended sizes may have different costs.

Can I supply my own garments?

We do not automatically accept customer-supplied garments. Ask first so we can assess material compatibility and production risk.

Do you offer embroidery?

Embroidery is not currently offered. Our primary decoration service is full-color DTF printing.

Can I request a rush order?

Rush requests are reviewed case by case, based on current workload, garment availability and the deadline. Rush fees may apply.

How do I pay?

Payment is required in full before production begins. Your invoice and payment instructions are provided after the order is approved.

Do you ship?

Shipping is available for applicable orders and is quoted separately. Ask us about local pickup and delivery options.

Can I get the artwork file?

We accept your artwork uploads. A digital apparel mockup for approval is included; original design or vector file creation is a separate service if needed.
